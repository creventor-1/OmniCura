1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OmniCura is a production-grade, privacy-preserving clinical AI triage and healthcare
intelligence platform. It bridges the gap between conversational AI diagnostics and
cryptographic data integrity.

Traditional medical AI systems present severe privacy and trust risks:
1. Centralized Data Exposure: Sensitive personal health information (PHI) is
   frequently logged, mined, or sold.
2. Undetected Record Alteration: Intermediaries, compromised databases, or rogue
   actors can silently modify triage urgency, diagnoses, or care plans.
3. Lack of Patient Custody: Patients rarely own or control access to their records.

OmniCura solves this by deploying a dual-layer architecture:
- Layer 1 (Clinical AI Layer): Rapid, adaptive multi-turn symptom assessment,
  visual symptom analysis, pharmaceutical recognition, and emergency routing.
- Layer 2 (Trust & Integrity Layer): Cryptographic canonical fingerprinting and
  Zcash blockchain anchoring, guaranteeing patient data privacy through zero-
  knowledge principles while rendering medical records permanently tamper-evident.


2. CORE CLINICAL MODULES & CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Symptom Assessment (Adaptive Clinical Triage)
- Guided conversational interface powered by Google Gemini AI.
- Adaptive Questioning: Instead of producing an immediate guess, OmniCura
  dynamically asks relevant follow-up questions tailored to reported symptoms
  (e.g., onset, pain scale, radiation, breathing triggers, travel history).
- Red-Flag Interceptor: Real-time screening for life-threatening emergencies
  (cardiac arrest, stroke, anaphylaxis, severe sepsis). If red flags appear,
  it immediately halts chat and triggers high-urgency SOS dialing protocols.
- Acuity Verdict: Classifies condition into "Emergency", "Urgent", or "Non-Urgent"
  with a clinical reasoning breakdown and confidence scoring.
- First Aid & Care Guidance: Immediate actionable first-aid steps and explicit
  warning signs indicating when to escalate to an emergency room.

B. Symptom Vision (Multimodal Visual Triage)
- Direct image analysis for visible dermatological conditions, rashes, burns, cuts,
  wound infections, and swelling.
- Uses high-resolution computer vision models to evaluate morphological features,
  tissue coloration, and lesion boundaries.

C. Medication Scanner (Pharmaceutical Intelligence)
- Real-time optical scanning of medicine boxes, blister packs, and pharmacy labels.
- Extracts active pharmaceutical ingredients, recommended dosages, contraindications,
  and potential interactions with the patient's existing medications.

D. Care Network & Hospital Locator
- Geolocation-based facility finder mapped via OpenStreetMap / Leaflet.
- Locates nearest verified emergency departments, trauma centers, and urgent clinics.
- Displays driving distance, direct emergency dispatch phone numbers, and turn-by-turn
  navigation routes.

E. Persistent Sovereign Health Profile
- Securely stores patient biological sex, age, blood group, known allergies, chronic
  conditions, and current medications.
- The AI automatically incorporates profile context into triage assessments without
  forcing the user to re-type repetitive medical background.

F. Medical History & Encounters Timeline
- Searchable and filterable longitudinal timeline of all past assessments.
- Supports PDF export, clinical summaries for physicians, and record deletion.


3. THE BLOCKCHAIN & PRIVACY VERIFICATION ARCHITECTURE (ZCASH)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The critical philosophy of OmniCura is:
"Sensitive healthcare data must remain 100% private, while authenticity, ownership,
and record integrity must be verifiable by anyone."

A. Zero Personal Health Information (PHI) On-Chain
- At no point are patient names, symptoms, medical history, photos, or diagnoses
  written to the blockchain.
- All clinical data remains in the patient's private encrypted local storage or
  secure database with Row-Level Security (RLS).
- Only cryptographic hashes (fingerprints) and zero-knowledge attestation memos
  interact with the Zcash network.

B. Canonical JSON Fingerprinting (Universal SHA-256 Engine)
To ensure that an assessment produces the exact same hash regardless of operating
system, client, or JSON key order, OmniCura uses deterministic canonicalization:
1. Normalization: Whitespace is trimmed; strings are normalized.
2. Canonical Key Sorting: JSON keys are lexicographically sorted prior to hashing:
   - patientId
   - symptoms
   - severity (emergency | urgent | non-urgent)
   - recommendedAction
   - possibleConditions (sorted alphabetically)
   - firstAidSteps
   - warningSigns
   - timestamp (ISO-8601 UTC)
   - schemaVersion ("omnicura-v1")
3. Hashing: Computed through a deterministic SHA-256 digest yielding a 64-character
   hexadecimal fingerprint (e.g., 780ddddd2b9b9f561a0e4acef8f0474ba6399a38...).

C. Zcash Testnet Notary Attestation & Shielded Memo Proofs
Once the canonical hash is generated:
1. Shielded Memo Payload:
   A standard zero-knowledge attestation payload is constructed:
   "omnicura:v1:assessment:<record_sha256_hash>:<timestamp>"
2. Dual-Proof Model:
   - Shielded Proof: For zero-knowledge confidentiality, the attestation memo is
     transmitted within a shielded Zcash transaction.
   - On-Chain Transaction Anchor: The notary wallet broadcasts a real shielded
     self-transfer carrying the memo. The txid the network returns is stored
     with the record, and its block height is read back from the chain, never
     assumed. Example: txid
     18bcad373b1241c6730a6637f40e19eb329a8c74336f2bd314a0132a0fd4ea58,
     confirmed in Zcash testnet block #4618838.
   - Consent events (access grants, revocations, share invitations, clinician
     requests, approvals and denials) are anchored the same way with the memo
     "omnicura:v1:consent:<event_sha256_hash>:<timestamp>".
3. Block Explorer Integration:
   Each assessment displays a live verification badge linking to public block
   explorers (SoChain / Blockchair) so judges, clinicians, or patients can verify
   on-chain timestamping.

D. Unified Address Support (ZIP 316 / Bech32m)
OmniCura supports modern Zcash standards:
- Modern Unified Address (UA): Starts with "utest1..." (e.g.,
  utest19d569wa8aaggfuvmvuf26808wdhvx74c7ava3ggxqxzd43hsm4ter3zltvumx463wq5rwy4jnd89klc0pgv7rp2u8498wmanhyhc5m79).
  Bundles Orchard and Sapling shielded pools with transparent receivers.
- Zero-Knowledge Shielded Mode: In accordance with Zcash privacy principles,
  shielded balances are private by design and invisible to public web scrapers,
  verifiable only by wallets holding the viewing key (e.g., YWallet, Zashi).
- Transparent Fallback: Also supports transparent P2PKH addresses ("tm...") for
  direct faucet integration and transparent notary scripts.

E. Cryptographic Tamper Detection
- How it Works: Whenever an assessment is opened, OmniCura reconstructs the
  canonical assessment payload from the current record and re-runs SHA-256.
- Tamper Trigger: If even a single character has been changed (for example, if a
  malicious party alters the severity from "urgent" to "non-urgent" to deny care),
  the SHA-256 avalanche effect produces a completely different hash.
- Instant Alert: The UI immediately switches from "Zcash Verified" to a bright red
  alert: "TAMPERING DETECTED: Hash Mismatch".
- Hackathon Interactive Demonstration: The UI includes a "Simulate Tampering" /
  "Restore Original Record" toggle so judges and users can observe tamper detection
  in real time.

F. Patient Data Ownership & Access Control System
Located in the "Data Ownership" workspace (#ownership):
1. Sovereign Custody: The patient is recognized as the sovereign owner of the record.
2. Granting Access: Patients can grant "Full Clinical Read" or "Emergency Only" access
   to hospitals, specialists, or primary physicians (e.g., Dr. Sarah Chen).
3. Instant Revocation: Patients can revoke access with one click, locking the record.
4. Verifiable Audit Trail: Every creation, grant, and revocation generates an immutable
   cryptographic event hash and transaction reference displayed on a chronological
   timeline.

5. END-TO-END PATIENT WORKFLOW WALKTHROUGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Patient Encounter
The user opens OmniCura and enters symptoms:
"High fever 103F since yesterday, severe pounding headache, and light hurts my eyes."

Step 2: AI Triage & Follow-up
The AI checks for red flags, then asks targeted follow-ups:
"Is your neck stiff when touching your chin to your chest? Did symptoms start suddenly?"

Step 3: Synthesis & Acuity Verdict
The AI synthesizes the encounter:
- Urgency: "Urgent"
- Possible Conditions: Complicated Bacterial Sinusitis (84%), Atypical Migraine (62%)
- Recommendation: Consult an urgent care clinician within 2 hours.
- First Aid: Rest in a dark room, hydrate, avoid bright lights.

Step 4: Cryptographic Hashing
The application instantly compiles the canonical JSON payload and computes its SHA-256
hash: e.g. 780ddddd2b9b9f561a0e4acef8f0474ba6399a38202deb424dd31583ec8af7a9.

Step 5: Zcash Blockchain Anchoring
The server recomputes the hash itself, and the notary sidecar sends a shielded Zcash
testnet transaction carrying it in the memo. It returns the real txid, e.g.
18bcad373b1241c6730a6637f40e19eb329a8c74336f2bd314a0132a0fd4ea58
(https://sochain.com/tx/ZECTEST/18bcad373b1241c6730a6637f40e19eb329a8c74336f2bd314a0132a0fd4ea58).

Step 6: Patient Review & Verification Badge
The badge reads "Anchoring on Zcash" until the transaction is mined, then
"Anchored on Zcash · Block #N" with a direct link to the testnet block explorer.
If the record is later edited, its recomputed hash no longer matches and the badge
turns to "Tampered".

Step 7: Physician Access Grant
The patient visits the Data Ownership panel and clicks "Grant Provider Access" to
authorize "Dr. Elena Rostova (Neurology)". An ownership grant event hash is generated,
recorded on the audit timeline, and anchored in its own Zcash testnet transaction.

Step 8: Independent Verification
The physician receives the record. OmniCura verifies the clinical data against the
Zcash anchor. The physician confirms the record is authentic and unaltered since creation.
